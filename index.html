Scale the array, not the wiring.

Alienonyx designs cryogenic analog and mixed-signal ASICs — starting with commercial integrated readout for superconducting single-photon detector arrays.

Get in touch

Superconducting detectors and quantum hardware are scaling faster than the electronics that read them out. Every channel still costs dedicated coax lines up to room temperature, a discrete amplifier chain, and a share of a hard 4 K heat budget. Research ASICs have shown that integrated cold readout works — but a demonstration is not a component. Alienonyx exists to close that gap: readout silicon you can actually design in — a documented, supported, purchasable part with an engineer behind it.

Where we are. Alienonyx is pre-silicon, deliberately in the open. Our first test chip is planned on IHP’s open shuttle, and v1 requirements are being set now — with design-in partners. Early partners shape the spec and get first access, at no cost or commitment.
